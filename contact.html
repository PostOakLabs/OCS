Support the research

The Omega Centauri Society is organised as a research programme. Affiliation at any level supports telescope time, grant proposals and the public case for studying Omega Centauri as an astrophysical and SETI target.

Affiliate: Open enrolment, no fee. Research bulletin, open-access publications, annual public lecture, supporter register.

Associate: Annual contribution. Funds telescope time, paper acknowledgements, working group access, pre-publication data.

Principal: Major grant, by arrangement. Named fellowship, co-applicant on telescope proposals, advisory board seat.

Write to the address above with the topic line “membership”.

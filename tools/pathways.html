Where to start

Two routes through the site depending on what you're here for. Each step builds on the last — follow the chain, or jump to any step directly.

New visitor · student

I want to understand the evidence from scratch

Start with the story, then work through the tools that quantify it.

1
Narrative · scrollytelling
Is There an IMBH?
The full argument in five sections — anomaly, kinematics, energy, detectability, verdict. Live tools embedded as you scroll.
2
Dashboard · live
IMBH Evidence Dashboard
Adjust mass, spin, and magnetic field once — all six observational indicators update simultaneously.
3
Tool · kinematics
IMBH Constraint Stacker
Toggle published measurements on and off to see how the allowed mass window changes. The tension is visible here.
4
Tool · energy
BZ–Kardashev Power
If the IMBH is spinning, how much energy can it extract? Slide spin and field strength to see Blandford-Znajek power in real time.
5
Hub · meta
Falsification & Observational Roadmap
What observations would prove or disprove each claim? The instruments, timescales, and predicted signatures — if it has no entry, it isn't science yet.
Researcher · scientist

Show me the evidence and the open questions fast

Start at the constraints, work toward the formal proposals.

1
Dashboard · live
IMBH Evidence Dashboard
Multi-constraint overview — BZ power, radio flux, gamma signal, Penrose reservoir, spin-up timeline, mass tension. Adjust parameters directly.
2
Tool · kinematics
IMBH Constraint Stacker
Full published constraint landscape — kinematics, proper motions, pulsar timing, accretion, N-body, M-σ. Stacked on a single mass axis.
3
Hub · meta
Falsification & Observational Roadmap
Structured map of every testable prediction — the instrument, expected signal, and timeline. Covers IMBH, MTH, and Fermi claims.
4
Workflow · staged
Closing the Constraint Window
Multi-stage calculator that walks through how each upcoming observation narrows the allowed mass range — outputs carry forward between stages.
5
Proposals · formal
Observational Proposals
Eleven working-draft proposals — neutrino, gamma-ray, radio, infrared, astrometric, and gravitational-wave channels. Each links to its relevant tools.
6
Tool cluster · CNS
Civilisational Cosmology
Three linked tools — Cosmological Natural Selection (Smolin & Vidal), MonkeyGod Monte Carlo (Stenger), and the CNS Fitness Landscape (hyperfecundity search) — plus Gough's JWST prediction tracker. Where does OC's IMBH fit in the reproductive history of the multiverse?

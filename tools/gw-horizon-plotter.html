Physics & citations

Characteristic strain for a circular inspiral (sky/polarisation averaged, Cutler & Flanagan 1994): h_c(f) = 2√(5/24)/π^{2/3} · c/d · (GM_c/c³)^{5/6} · f^{-1/6} where M_c = (m₁m₂)^{3/5}/(m₁+m₂)^{1/5} is the chirp mass. Track is cut off at the ISCO frequency.

ISCO frequency (Bardeen 1972, prograde equatorial): f_ISCO = c³/(πGM) / ((r_ISCO/r_g)^{3/2} + χ) where χ is the dimensionless spin and r_ISCO/r_g follows from the Kerr ISCO formulae.

Sensitivity curves are analytic approximations (see below). LISA uses the Robson, Cornish & Liu 2019 parameterisation; ET / CE / aLIGO use simplified power-law fits normalised to published design PSDs; DECIGO uses a simplified Yagi & Seto 2011 model; PTA shows approximate SKA-era single-source sensitivity.

⚠ Häberle 2024 (≥ 8,200 M☉ lower bound) and Bañares-Hernández 2025 (≤ 6,000 M☉ 3σ upper bound) are both valid and unreconciled. The dual dashed tracks preserve this tension.

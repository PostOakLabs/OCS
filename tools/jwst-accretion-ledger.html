JWST Accretion
Ledger

Model-dependent

Computes the IMBH mass above which predicted Bondi/ADAF accretion luminosity would exceed the JWST NIRCam/MIRI bolometric upper limit for Omega Centauri's core. This is a third, independent constraint axis alongside stellar kinematics and pulsar timing.

Model-dependent result The exclusion scales with the assumed ADAF radiative efficiency epsilon. This bound is NOT a peer of the Haberle et al. 2024 kinematic lower bound (≥ 8,200 M-sun) or the Banares-Hernandez et al. 2025 pulsar timing upper bound (≤ 6,000 M-sun). Chen et al. 2025 (arXiv:2511.20945) constrains (mass, accretion) combinations; it does not report a ~20,000 M-sun detection.

What this tool computes

      
Given an ADAF radiative efficiency epsilon, ambient gas density rho_inf, and the JWST NIRCam/MIRI bolometric luminosity upper limit (fixed at 10²⁸ W, Chen et al. 2025), solves for the maximum IMBH mass consistent with non-detection:

      
M_limit = sqrt(L_limit * c_s³ / (epsilon * 4*pi*G² * rho_inf * c²)) / M_sun

      
where c_s = 10 km/s (approximate GC-core sound speed). The Bondi accretion model assumes spherical symmetry, steady state, and the ADAF radiative prescription.

      
Constraint axes (all three)

      
    
        
  • Kinematics (peer-reviewed): Haberle et al. 2024 — IMBH ≥ 8,200 M-sun
    • 
        
  • Pulsar timing (peer-reviewed): Banares-Hernandez et al. 2025 — IMBH ≤ 6,000 M-sun
    • 
        
  • Accretion (model-dependent): this tool — depends on epsilon, rho_inf
    • 
      

      
The kinematic and timing bounds are in tension. This accretion bound adds a third, independent axis but carries additional model uncertainty.

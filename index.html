One cluster, three questions

The site keeps two registers apart. Measurements of NGC 5139 come from peer-reviewed work and carry their citations. The Macro Transcension Hypothesis is speculative engineering and is labeled that way wherever it appears.

What is in the cluster?

Omega Centauri holds about ten million stars, several distinct stellar populations and a candidate intermediate-mass black hole. Two current bounds disagree: stellar kinematics give at least 8,200 M☉ (Häberle et al. 2024) while pulsar timing gives at most 6,000 M☉ (Bañares-Hernández et al. 2025).

The cluster →

What does the hypothesis claim?

That a civilization optimizing for computation would move inward, toward dense stellar systems and black hole power, rather than outward across the galaxy. The five phases, the energy argument and the computronium designs are worked out in one place.

The hypothesis →

How could it be wrong?

Each claim maps to an observation that would weaken or rule it out, from pulsar accelerations to infrared waste heat. The roadmap lists the instruments, the thresholds and the proposals already drafted for them.

Research programme

Advance the Science

The Omega Centauri Society is organised as a research programme, not a membership club. Affiliation at any level is an act of participation in a scientific project: funding telescope time, seeding grant proposals, and building the public case for taking Omega Centauri seriously as an object of SETI and astrophysical investigation.

Affiliate
Open enrolment · no fee. Research bulletin, open-access publications, annual public lecture, supporter register.
Associate
Annual contribution. Funds telescope time, paper acknowledgements, working group access, pre-publication data.
Principal
Major grant · by arrangement. Named fellowship, co-applicant on telescope proposals, advisory board seat.
